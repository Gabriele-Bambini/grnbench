# EXP-20261022-GB-2: Exploratory stress test - on the 80 exploratory datasets of the D-057 CELLxGENE pool, where does cleaning beat the fixed baseline under added doublets and damaged cells?

## 1. Registration (before the run)

- type: **exploratory** (descriptive; it cannot back a claim or change a default). Registered before any
  dataset of the list below is scored.
- owner: GB (agent-prepared)   issue: #140 (data: #141)   registered: 2026-10-22
- revised 2026-10-23, before any dataset was scored, after LM's and LD's reviews of the registration PR:
  the pool was redrawn without the datasets and studies of EXP-20261001-LD-1 (see EXP-20261022-GB-1
  section 1), the scope is the registered pool, subgroup breakdowns are declared, provenance is an
  experiment-specific data snapshot, and data and large outputs live in a dedicated data folder.
- follows: EXP-20261002-LM-2 (every cleaning candidate on 20 real datasets x 7 degradations: little
  headroom; without a reference the engine's choice lost to the baseline) and EXP-20261003-LM-1 (C7,
  confirmatory, 20 datasets: the baseline beat the former reference-free rule). The owner asked for a
  stress test on many datasets (2026-10-22). The pool and its split are D-057 (ACTIVE).
- scope: the 80 exploratory datasets of the registered pool (one per study, the smallest H5AD of each,
  at most 2 GB, 500 to 500,000 cells, human or mouse, mostly 10x 3'); not every scRNA-seq dataset.
- questions: under each condition (clean; 20% added doublets; 15% damaged cells): (1) how much do the
  selection rules (the former reference-free rule, the shipped rule = baseline since D-021, the
  reference-screened choice, the gene-set-preserving choice) change recovery against the fixed baseline;
  (2) which one-change variants of the baseline help or hurt, on their own genes and on the genes they
  share with the baseline; (3) the two comparisons of C7 (`candidate_study.CONFIRM_FAMILY`), recomputed
  here as a descriptive check of C7 and D-021.
- expectation (stated now): as in EXP-20261002-LM-2 and C7, small gains at best; the baseline at least as
  good as the reference-free rule in every condition.
- endpoint: `eval_mean` = AUPRC ratio on the CollecTRI evaluation half, mean of pearson_signed,
  spearman_signed and clr (`engine_bench.EVAL_METHODS`), as in EXP-20261002-LM-2 and C7.
- descriptive breakdowns (declared now): for question (3), the mean paired difference with its 95%
  interval in each subgroup of organism, assay family (10x 3', 10x 5', 10x multiome, full-length, other)
  and suspension (cell, nucleus, both), by the same script as EXP-20261022-GB-1, committed before the
  report is generated. Composition of the 80: 70 human / 10 mouse; 10x 3' 59, 10x 5' 8, 10x multiome 6,
  full-length 2, other 5; 22 single-nucleus.
- unit of analysis: dataset (planned n = 80 per condition). Never edges, cells or contexts.
- datasets: the 80 datasets of `experiments/EXP-20261022-GB-2.datasets.tsv`, the exploratory part of the
  D-057 pool (selection and split as in EXP-20261022-GB-1 section 1). The 80: 40.4 GB, 12 files above
  1 GB, 11 assays.
- conditions: `clean`, `doublets_20`, `damaged_15` (`candidate_study.CONDITIONS`; the three of C7).
- reference: the CollecTRI files of C4 and C7's registered snapshot, sha256 in
  `experiments/EXP-20261022-GB-1.snapshot.tsv` (cisTarget is not used here).
- exclusion rules (fixed now): (a) data failing `data_manifest.py --check` stop the run; (b) a dataset
  refused at ingest as not raw counts is reported and not scored; (c) datasets with fewer than 50
  reference edges are not scored; (d) a task where a candidate fails keeps the other candidates, as the
  runner does; a comparison leaves out datasets where either side has no score, and says how many;
  (e) tasks still missing after at most three 24 h jobs of the same commit are reported as missing, with
  the dataset size; (f) no other exclusion.
- comparisons: everything printed by `candidate_study.py report` (`SUMMARY.md`) and `confirm`
  (`CONFIRM.md`). The p-values of `CONFIRM.md` are descriptive here (exploratory record): nothing is
  "supported" or "refuted" by this run.
- seeds: degradation seed 0; reference split seed 0; the adapters' default seeds (as C7).

## 2. How it runs

- code: the same commit as EXP-20261022-GB-1 (branch `gb/pool-200`, cluster checkout
  `~/grnbench-pool200`, `CODE_COMMIT` copied into the results).
- environment: conda env `grnbench` on the cluster, Python 3.11, `requirements/lock-py311.txt`.
- storage: as in EXP-20261022-GB-1 (`~/grnbench_pool200`, `~/gb_runs`, `~/gb_tmp`; the scratch area is not
  writable for this account and home has no quota); only portable task files are committed.
- data (login node, downloads and checks only; `R=~/grnbench_pool200/refs` as in
  EXP-20261022-GB-1, `D=~/grnbench_pool200/exploratory`):
  ```
  python benchmarks/fetch_cellxgene.py --out $D/cellxgene --from-manifest experiments/EXP-20261022-GB-2.datasets.tsv
  python benchmarks/data_manifest.py $D > experiments/EXP-20261022-GB-2.data.tsv     # committed and pushed before any run
  python benchmarks/data_manifest.py $D --check experiments/EXP-20261022-GB-2.data.tsv --require-all   # must exit 0 at launch
  ```
  The data snapshot must hold exactly the 80 datasets' `data.h5ad` and `truth.json` (160 files).
- pilot: the pilot of EXP-20261022-GB-1 section 2 (three datasets of this exploratory part, condition
  `clean`); its scores are deleted unread and it only sets `JOBS`, the memory and the expected time.
- commands (`O=~/gb_runs/EXP-20261022-GB-2`; QOS `stud`, 8 CPUs, 24 h per job, memory from the
  pilot, queued after EXP-20261022-GB-1):
  ```
  python benchmarks/candidate_study.py plan --data-root $D --out $O/tasks.json --conditions clean,doublets_20,damaged_15
  sbatch --time=24:00:00 --mem=<from the pilot> --export=ALL,REPO_DIR=$HOME/grnbench-pool200,OUT=$O,SCRIPT=benchmarks/candidate_study.py,TASKS=$O/tasks.json,REFS=$R/references,CHUNK=100000,JOBS=<from the pilot>,TMPDIR=$HOME/gb_tmp hpc/engine_bench.sbatch
  python benchmarks/candidate_study.py report --out $O
  python benchmarks/candidate_study.py confirm --out $O
  ```
  240 tasks; the runner resumes unfinished tasks when the same job is resubmitted (at most three jobs).
- machine: Bocconi HPC, partition/QOS `stud`, 8 CPUs, 24 h per job.
- results: `benchmarks/results/EXP-20261022-GB-2/` (rows, `candidates.csv`, `SUMMARY.md`, `CONFIRM.md`,
  portable task file).
- figures: `candidate_study.py` writes tables, not figures; none are planned (said again in section 3).
- status at registration: no dataset of the list scored.
- status at launch (2026-10-23): all 80 datasets fetched with `--from-manifest` into the data folder;
  sha256 of their 160 files (`data.h5ad`, `truth.json`; exactly the list) in
  `experiments/EXP-20261022-GB-2.data.tsv`; references and cisTarget checked against the snapshot
  (ok=8, MISMATCH=0, MISSING=0). No model has run on any of them. The run commit is the one that adds
  this line.
