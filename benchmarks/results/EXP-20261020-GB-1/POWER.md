## Notes

- Levels: alpha/4 = first Holm step of C4's family of 4 directed hypotheses; alpha/3 = the same family once the other pair is rejected first; alpha/2 = a family of one pair (two directions).
- A signed-rank test instead of the t (not the registered test) would need about 106 (normal differences) and at most 117 datasets where the t needs 101 (auto, +0.10, alpha/2; asymptotic relative efficiency 3/pi and its minimum 0.864).
- `reference_edges` counts CollecTRI pairs of the scored TFs over all genes in both halves; it is not the k of the EPR. k, prevalence, the 1/prevalence ceiling and ties at k need the kept artifacts (step 15-2).
